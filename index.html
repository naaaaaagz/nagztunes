I am Nagz, a friendly xennial from Budapest, HU

I've been making music on the computer since 1996 and I don't seem to get bored of it. In the early days the demoscene accepted me as one of them, and as a method of payback, I made a metric f*ckton of chiptunes in FT2 because I found limitations sexy - plus I didn't have proper samples to begin with...

In 2014 I became a member of Maniacs Of Noise, the same year as the release of my first music video called 'Told You So'. Fast forward 6 years, I happened to finish my first LP called 'Good' on my live streams, which I consider a huge success, knowing how inconsistent my stylistic choices can be.

I am still an avid user of Renoise and it doesn't seem to change any time soon.

Please enjoy my stuff. This is far from everything I've ever made, but you deserve only the best, my boo!
